Drifton

Generative functional music in the browser, to focus or relax. The long-term goal: an infinite stream that adapts to you. Each part below explores one direction.

Composition · step 5

Generator

Press play and let it run. Four instruments played by rules: a pad holding voice-led chords, a bass, sparse bells wandering on the scale, and a texture. Pick the key, the mode, the pace.

Synth

Play the four instruments (Pad, Bass, Bells, Texture) from your keyboard and shape their sound: oscillators, noise, filter, envelopes, LFOs and modulation.
