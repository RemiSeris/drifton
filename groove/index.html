Groove

Rhythm styles written as grids of chances: each bar is drawn again from the grid, so the groove stays itself without ever repeating exactly. Pick two styles and morph from one to the other, like a band changing groove.

Gestures wait for the next beat, so they always land in time. For 1…6, use the top row of the keyboard (on AZERTY: & é " ' ( - without Shift) or the numeric keypad.

Play a melody

Octave 0

Free: you play. Guide: a melody is written for the groove, silently; its notes fall on the stage and light the keys ahead of time, and you play them (Perfect, Good, Miss). Auto: the melody plays itself. C switches. The two letter rows play the groove's scale, so there is no wrong note. Ringed keys: the root. Dotted keys: the three notes of the chord playing now, good places to land. The top row is the middle row an octave up.

Styles

Morph hands the instruments over one at a time, on the bar: hats, percussion, keys, bass, then the beat itself, announced by a drum fill while the tempo slides.

The chords' mode follows the style playing the keys: dorian for funk, aeolian for dub, major for bossa nova.

Instruments

Drums are synthesised (no samples). The styles live in groove/styles.js, as readable grids: edit one, refresh, listen.
